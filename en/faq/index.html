Frequently asked.

Is SuperFinder free?

Yes. Both the App Store build and the direct download from this site are free. The direct build includes the external plugin port, unlockable with a Pro key (¥38 one-time); the online purchase flow is under construction.

Do my files get uploaded anywhere?

No. SuperFinder itself has zero networking: no account, no cloud sync, no telemetry — files never leave your Mac. External plugins are code you installed yourself; their networking is yours.

What does it require?

SuperFinder needs macOS 14 or later, with a Simplified Chinese and English interface. Current version 1.6.0 (build 12, released 2026-10-17).

How does it relate to Finder?

SuperFinder works as your everyday file manager: address-bar jumps, tabs and split panes, a staging shelf and session restore. It interoperates with Finder's tags, QuickLook and permissions — no need to remove or replace Finder.

What's the difference between the direct and App Store builds?

The direct build includes the external plugin port (custom .sfplugin bundles); the sandboxed App Store build cannot. Everything else matches, and the direct DMG is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple.

Is the download safe?

Yes. The direct DMG is signed with a Developer ID, notarized and stapled by Apple, with an SHA-256 checksum you can verify yourself; the App Store build goes through Apple review.

Does it uninstall cleanly?

Yes. SuperFinder is a single .app you drag into /Applications, with no background daemons; delete the .app to uninstall. The login item is optional.
